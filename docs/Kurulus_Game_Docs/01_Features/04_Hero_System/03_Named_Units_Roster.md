# سجل الوحدات المسماة — Named Units Roster

> **Description:** Detailed roster of named units — unique non-hero characters with distinct abilities, story arcs, and combat roles. These units are not full heroes (no talent trees, no equipment) but have unique stats and barks that make them memorable.

---

## 1. System Overview

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Total Named Units** | 6 (5 playable + 1 boss NPC) |
| **Unlock Method** | Story events, mission objectives, hidden discoveries |
| **Persistence** | Per-mission (return in future missions if they survive) |
| **Permadeath** | Yes — if killed in combat, they are gone for the rest of the campaign |
| **Max on Field** | 3-4 per mission (balanced limit) |

### How Named Units Differ From Heroes

| Aspect | Heroes (Osman, Turgut, Buran) | Named Units |
|--------|-------------------------------|-------------|
| Talent Trees | ✅ Full (10 levels) | ❌ No |
| Equipment Slots | ✅ 3 (Weapon, Armor, Accessory) | ❌ No |
| Abilities | ✅ 4 (Q, W, E, R) | ✅ 1-2 unique passives |
| Story Importance | ✅ Main characters | ✅ Supporting characters |
| Replaceable | ❌ No (if die, return next mission) | ✅ Yes (can be replaced) |
| Voice Lines | 10+ per hero | 4-6 per unit |

## 2. Konur Alp — فارس الكايي الأول

### Basic Info

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Full Name** | Konur Alp bin [Father's Name] |
| **Title** | قائد فرسان الكايي |
| **Role** | Light Cavalry Commander |
| **Unlock** | Mission 2 (after uniting the tribe) |
| **Weapon** | Curved sword (Kılıç) + small round shield |

### Appearance
Konur is a young, energetic horseman in his mid-20s. He wears light leather armor optimized for riding — no heavy plates. A blue headband under his helmet. His horse is a fast Anatolian racer, smaller than heavy cavalry horses but agile. He has a permanent confident smirk.

### Personality
Konur is the eternal optimist of the tribe. Quick to laugh, quick to fight, slow to give up. He follows Osman not out of duty but out of genuine belief. Sometimes reckless — Turgut often has to pull him back. Loyal to a fault.

### Stats

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Health | 250 |
| Attack Damage | 22 (Slash) |
| Attack Speed | 1.3/sec |
| Armor | 15 |
| Move Speed | 5.2 m/s (on horse) |

### Unique Passive: **خفة الفارس (Rider's Swiftness)**
- +15% attack damage when charging from 10m+ distance
- +20% move speed for 5 seconds after killing an enemy

### Barks

| Trigger | Text |
|---------|------|
| Selection | "خيلي وسيفي — كلاهما في خدمتك!" |

## 3. Ayazbey — سهم الليل

### Basic Info

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Full Name** | Ayazbey bin [Unknown] |
| **Title** | قائد رماة الكايي |
| **Role** | Archer Commander |
| **Unlock** | Mission 3 (after the ambush) |
| **Weapon** | Compound bow + short dagger |

### Appearance
Ayazbey is lean and tall with sharp cheekbones. He wears practical hunter's clothes — greens and browns for camouflage. A hood is always up, shadowing his face. He has a calm, almost eerie stillness — even in battle, he never seems to hurry. Always has an arrow nocked.

### Personality
Ayazbey is the quiet professional. He speaks only when necessary, and when he does, it's worth hearing. He doesn't get excited or panicked — just calculates and acts. Buran considers him the only other "true archer" in the tribe. Mysterious past — no one knows where he came from before joining the Kayı.

### Stats

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Health | 180 |
| Attack Damage | 20 (Pierce) |
| Attack Speed | 1.6/sec |
| Armor | 10 |
| Move Speed | 4.5 m/s |

### Unique Passive: **عين الصقر (Hawk's Eye)**
- +25% range compared to regular archers
- Can spot stealth units at +5m distance (passive detection)

### Barks

## 6. Salur Alp — القائد المخلص

### Basic Info

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Full Name** | Salur Alp |
| **Title** | قائد فرقة الاستطلاع |
| **Role** | Scout Squad Leader |
| **Unlock** | Available from Mission 1 (story NPC) |
| **Weapon** | Sword + bow (versatile) |

### Appearance
Salur is a capable, no-nonsense soldier in his early 40s. Moderate build, practical gear, always functional never flashy. A scar runs across his left eyebrow from an old battle. He has a calm, reliable demeanor — never the hero, always there when needed.

### Personality
Salur is the ultimate middle-manager of the tribe's military. Not a legend like Turgut, not a prodigy like Konur — just solid, reliable, always where he's needed. He handles logistics, scouting, and communications. Osman trusts him with critical tasks.

### Stats

|------|-------|
| Health | 220 |
| Attack Damage | 20 (versatile — slash or pierce) |
| Attack Speed | 1.1/sec |
| Armor | 18 |
| Move Speed | 4.2 m/s |

### Unique Passive: **عين القبيلة (Tribe's Eye)**

- All units in his squad gain +5% move speed
